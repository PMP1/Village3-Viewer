# LPC character artwork credits

The walk-cycle PNG layers in `lpc/` are sourced from the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator:

https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator

They are used here under the **OGA-BY 3.0** option listed by their upstream definitions. The files have not been redrawn or converted; Village3 composes the layers directly at runtime.
